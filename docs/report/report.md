## Caveats

1. Weights come from a public mirror, not the gated official repo.
2. Reflection is local Qwen3-8B-AWQ, weaker than the paper's GPT-4.1-mini; a flat GEPA
   result is evidence about *this* configuration, not about GEPA in general.
3. GEPA ran at 1,500 metric calls, not the paper's budget; the curve shows where it stopped.
4. Both methods see GSM8K train (T2L's hypernetwork was trained on SNI and is applied
   zero-shot here; GEPA optimises on the train split), so exposure is symmetric but the
   benchmark itself may be contaminated in pretraining.
5. One run per greedy arm; CIs are bootstrap over items, not over seeds.
